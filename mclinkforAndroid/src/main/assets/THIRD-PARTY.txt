MCLink for Android bundles the EasyTier Android JNI library.

EasyTier is licensed under the GNU Lesser General Public License, version 3.
The corresponding source and build instructions are available at:
https://github.com/EasyTier/EasyTier/tree/81487c363a56779e745eae927e130a813535c71e
https://github.com/EasyTier/EasyTier/tree/81487c363a56779e745eae927e130a813535c71e/easytier-contrib/easytier-android-jni

The unmodified arm64-v8a library comes from EasyTierCompose v1.0.10:
https://github.com/originalFactor/EasyTierCompose/tree/v1.0.10/app/src/main/jniLibs/arm64-v8a

The MCLink Android build can use a replacement EasyTier JNI library by
updating the pinned source and checksum in the Android build scripts.
